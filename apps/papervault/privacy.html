Papervault — Privacy Policy

Effective 25 August 2026

Papervault is a document archive that lives on your device. It is built so that the developer of Papervault cannot see your documents. There is no Papervault cloud, no account with us, and no telemetry. The app talks only to storage you configure.

What Papervault collects

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. The developer operates no server that the app talks to.

Where your data lives

Your documents, the text recognised from them, and everything derived from them — the search index, the embeddings, the filing suggestions — are stored on your device. Text recognition, search and the on-device AI models all run locally.

What leaves your device, and only if you set it up

  • Sync to storage you own. If you connect a backend — Proton Drive, S3, WebDAV, SFTP, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or a plain folder — your documents replicate to that storage under your own account. With vault encryption enabled they are encrypted before upload, so the provider stores files it cannot read or name. That provider's privacy practices are its operator's.
  • An AI endpoint you choose. The assistant runs on-device by default. If you configure a remote OpenAI-compatible endpoint instead, the document text you ask about is sent to that endpoint — which, again, you chose and control.
  • Model downloads. On-device AI models are fetched from public model repositories when you install them. Nothing about you or your documents is sent with the download.
  • Paperless-ngx import. If you import an existing archive, the app reads from the server you point it at and writes nothing back to it.

Each of these is off until you turn it on.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — scanning documents. The images go into your archive and nowhere else.
  • Notifications — reminders you set, and the status of background work.
  • Biometrics — unlocking the app. Handled by your operating system; the app never sees your fingerprint or face data.

Children

Papervault is a general-purpose filing tool, not directed at children, and collects no data from anybody.

Your rights

Everything is on your device or in storage you control, so access, export and deletion are directly in your hands: export any document at any time, or delete the app and its data. There is no copy held by us to request, correct or erase.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version appears on this page with a new effective date. The app is open source, so the behaviour this policy describes can be checked against the code.
